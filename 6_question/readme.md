## 6. Assess an environmental application 

Read Hassard et al. (2024), Scaling-up Engineering Biology for Enhanced Environmental Solutions. ACS Synthetic Biology 13, 1586–1588. DOI 10.1021/acssynbio.4c00292. 

Read Lea-Smith et al. (2025), Engineering biology applications for environmental solutions: potential and challenges. Nature Communications 16, 3538. DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-58492-0. 

1. Explain the central argument of each paper in your own words. Identify where their priorities agree and where the later paper adds detail or evidence. 

2. Choose one application discussed in the papers. Define the environmental problem, the proposed biological intervention and a measurable criterion for success. 

3. Prepare a comparison table linking the expected benefit to the evidence available, the main obstacle to scaling, and the next test needed. Include ecological interactions, containment, cost and relevant stakeholder concerns where they affect your chosen application. 

4. Discuss your analysis with another pair. Distinguish demonstrated results from proposals, challenge one assumption and revise your recommendation in light of the discussion. 

5. Keep a one-page comparison with citations, a justified next experiment or study, and one question the papers leave unresolved.

Represent the lecture circuits with SBOL Visual

Start with the official SBOL overview and SBOL Visual guidance:
1. Find out what the Synthetic Biology Open Language (SBOL) records and how SBOL Visual represents a design. 
Explain the difference in two or three sentences.
2. Use SBOL Visual to draw the lecture circuits: the IPTG reporter, AHL sender and receiver, inverter, NOR gate, 
toggle switch, negative autoregulation and repressilator. You may reuse shared components across diagrams.
3. Label the parts, their orientation and the regulatory interactions. State the host dependencies, including 
where LacI is supplied. Keep DNA order distinct from activation or repression.
4. Exchange diagrams with another student. Independently read the input, output and mechanism from each 
diagram, then check the glyphs against the standard. Resolve disagreements and record the corrections.
Keep your labelled diagrams, a brief explanation of SBOL and SBOL Visual, and the changes made after 
comparison

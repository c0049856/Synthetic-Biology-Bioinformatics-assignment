The biological question: When a microbial community receives a newcomer, either as an invader or mutated cell during the cell division, how likely is it, that the newcomer perish or survive? How likely is it, that it will grow to a decent-sized population (or die out), and how long would it take? And if the immigrants stop coming, how long is it until the population disappears?

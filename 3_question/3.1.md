DNA yield - total mass/quantity of DNA obtained after PCR, calculated by multiplying concentration by a total volume of the purified sample. High yield tells you how much of the DNA you have, but that does not mean that the material is clean. DNA yield is measured in µg. 

DNA purity - tells how clean the DNA sample is, and whether it has proteins, RNA, phenol, detergents and all other contaminants. Usually evaluated using UV absorbance ratios via spectrophotometer: 
- A260/A280 ratio measures protein/RNA contamination, and pure double-stranded DNA would typically give ratio of 1.7 to 2.0. Lower ratio means protein contamination. 
- A260/A230 ratio determines contamination from salts, EDTA< phenol, carbohydrates. Optimal ratio typically must be greater than 1.5, and pure dsDNA typically has 2.0-2.4. 
Overall DNA purity can be calculated using following:
DNA purity (A260/A280) = (A260 reading – A320 reading) ÷ (A280 reading – A320 reading).

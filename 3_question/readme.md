## 3. Explore PCR planning with the virtual simulator 

Read Fellermann et al. (2019), Design of experiments and the virtual PCR simulator: An online game for pharmaceutical scientists and biotechnologists. Pharmaceutical Statistics 18, 402–406. DOI 10.1002/pst.1932. 

1. Open the simulator introduction and access links 

2. Familiarise yourself with the controls and with the meanings of DNA yield and purity. Run a baseline reaction and record every setting and both outputs. 

3. Choose two adjustable factors, such as annealing temperature and polymerase amount. Predict their effects, then test a low and a high value of each in all four combinations, keeping the other settings fixed. 

4. Compare yield and purity across the runs. Identify whether the effect of one factor depends on the other. Choose a promising condition, test it again and explain what you would try next. 

5. Write a short PCR plan justified by your results. State which features the simplified simulator omits and which choices would still need checking in a real reaction. 

6. Keep a settings and results table, one comparison plot and a short explanation of your proposed PCR conditions.
